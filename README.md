🎓 BPIR GA Dashboard – Education & Employment Insights
Overview

This project was developed as part of the BPIR Graduate Assistant Data Challenge. The goal was to design a responsive and interactive dashboard that visualizes key insights from higher education completions and labor market employment data.

The dashboard was built using React, Plotly.js, and Material UI, focusing on clarity, storytelling, and user interactivity for institutional and workforce planning use cases.

🧩 Datasets Used

The dashboard integrates five worksheets selected from the BPIR GA dataset, categorized under Education and Employment themes.

🎓 Education Section

Completions by Institution

Metrics: total completions, tuition fees, market share, and year-over-year growth

Visuals: horizontal bar charts for institution-level comparisons

Insights: compare trends across universities such as UF, UT Austin, etc.

Regional Completions by Award Level

Data: completion trends by degree type (certificate, associate’s, bachelor’s)

Visuals: donut chart with checkbox filters

Insights: distribution of completions across award levels

💼 Employment Section

Job Postings by Top Companies

Focus: most active companies based on total and unique postings

Visuals: bar chart comparing posting counts and median durations

Example leaders: Randstad, Jobot

Target Occupations

Metrics: annual openings, job counts, earnings, and growth rate

Visuals: scatter or bar charts highlighting high-demand roles

Insights: occupations like Home Health Aides and General Managers rank high in growth potential

Top Cities for Job Postings

Data: job posting activity across U.S. cities

Visuals: horizontal bar chart

Insights: New York, Chicago, and Atlanta lead in total postings and job diversity

🛠️ Tech Stack

Frontend Framework: React.js

Data Visualization: Plotly.js

UI Library: Material UI

Deployment: GitHub Pages

🚀 Key Features

Interactive Filters: Real-time selection by award level, institution, and city

Responsive Layout: Adapts smoothly to desktop and mobile devices

Insightful Design: Combines institutional completions with labor market trends for planning and policy evaluation
